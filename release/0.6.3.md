# 0.6.3 - In-app browsers, class grants and print fixes

## Visiting from in-app browsers

Opening scvmrack from inside apps such as Reddit or Facebook now works for the current visit even when their browser discards session cookies.

- **Warning and link** — a persistent banner explains that your scvm will be lost when that browser closes and offers a **Copy link** button so you can open scvmrack in Safari or Chrome to keep it. Regular browser sessions work as before.

## Fixes

- **Class grants** — the Heretical Priest now gets the Shepherd's Crook and Poltroon the Court Jester gets his pet, as their class abilities say. Both were silently dropped before.
- **Print sheet** — the Modifiers section is now translated, and long ability labels such as Wytrzymałość no longer overflow the stats row.
- **Always the latest version** — returning visitors no longer boot a stale cached copy of the app after a release.
- **Quieter monitoring** — dropped-connection errors from flaky networks are no longer reported.

# Scvm Rack — v0.4.2

**Release date:** 2026-06-24
**Type:** Patch — lantern/torch classification fix

A small data fix so torches and the lantern behave like the consumables they are.

---

## Highlights

- **Lanterns and torches are consumables again.** They now show up in the
  Consumables section with their use pips and count toward encumbrance, instead
  of being misfiled as ammunition.
- **Localized sourcebook link.** On the Polish view, the "Get the sourcebook"
  button now points to the Polish edition at nerdsirens.pl/morkborg.
- **Translation credit.** The footer now credits the official Polish translation
  by Nerd Sirens.

## Under the hood

- The catalog gave the lantern and torches a stray `ammo_type` ("Oil" / "Torch")
  — a refill label already carried in their use-effect. Both the UI and the
  backend treat anything with an ammo type as ammunition, which dropped them from
  the Consumables list and exempted them from encumbrance. The fix clears that
  field in the catalog and strips it from existing characters' inventory, leaving
  real ammo (arrows/bolts) untouched.

## Verification

- Applied as a Prisma migration (existing databases) and a seed file (fresh
  databases); the lantern resolves into Consumables and counts toward encumbrance.

## English

### Contact

**Operator:** Adam Kościelniak, who runs Scvm Rack as a non-commercial hobby project within rpgtools.co.

**Email:** contact@rpgtools.co

We answer in Polish or English.

### For users

Write to contact@rpgtools.co with questions, complaints about the service, account removal or data requests. This address is our point of contact for users under Article 12 of the EU Digital Services Act.

- **Report a Custom Class:** use the "Report" link on the class, or the [report page](/report). No account is needed.
- **Dispute a moderation decision:** use the button on the notice in the app, or write to scvmrack-appeal@rpgtools.co.

### For authorities

contact@rpgtools.co is our single point of contact for:

- the authorities of EU member states, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (Article 11 of the Digital Services Act), and
- removal orders for terrorist content (Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2021/784).

We accept messages in Polish and English.

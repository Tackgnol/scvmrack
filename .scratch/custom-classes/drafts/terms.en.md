## 2. Words used here

- **Guest**: someone using the service without an account.
- **Account**: an rpgtools.co sign-in used with Scvm Rack.
- **GM**: a signed-in user who runs a party.
- **Custom Class**: a character class a GM writes in the class creator, with everything in it (text, abilities, Class Items, tables).
- **Public class**: a Custom Class its author has published, so other GMs can find it and use it.
- **Your content**: anything you enter: characters, notes, party names, Custom Classes, reports.

## 3. Using the service

- The service is free. There is no paid tier.
- You can start as a guest. A guest keeps one character for 7 days. Signing up keeps characters permanently and unlocks the GM tools, including Custom Classes.
- You need a current web browser with JavaScript and browser storage turned on, and an internet connection. The Owlbear Rodeo extension also needs an Owlbear Rodeo room.
- [OWNER] Minimum age: suggested "You must be at least 16, or have a parent's or guardian's consent" (the Polish GDPR consent age).
- These terms form an agreement between you and the operator when you first use the service. You can end it at any time by stopping using the service and, if you have an account, asking for its removal (§11).

## 4. Your content stays yours

You keep every right you have in your content. To run the service, you give the operator a free, non-exclusive, worldwide licence to store, copy, show and adapt your content (for example, to translate the interface around it or fit it on a phone screen), for as long as the service holds it.

For a **public class**, the licence also covers:

- showing it to every Scvm Rack user, and
- letting other GMs add it to their parties, and their players roll characters from it.

Characters are rolled from a fixed version of a class. A character already rolled from your class keeps that version's text, even if you later edit, unpublish, archive or delete the class. The licence continues for those characters for as long as they exist. You can't withdraw it from characters that were already rolled.

## 5. What you may not post

Don't post content that:

- is illegal, including child sexual abuse material, threats to anyone's life or safety, and terrorist content;
- you don't have the right to share, such as text copied from a book, zine or other work without the rightsholder's permission;
- harasses, threatens or demeans people, or is hateful;
- contains someone else's personal data without their consent.

Don't misuse the service: no automated mass requests, getting around limits, probing for weaknesses without permission, or impersonating others.

When you publish a class for the first time, you confirm that you made it or have the right to share it.

## 6. Publishing Custom Classes

- Only the author can publish their class, and only a saved and complete one.
- Publishing makes the latest version public at once. Later saves update the public class; GMs who use it always get the latest version, and characters already rolled keep theirs (§4).
- You can unpublish at any time. The class then leaves other GMs' class pools, and they see a notice. Characters already rolled from it keep it (§4).
- Zine and Rack classes are added by the operator. Zine authors who want their class on the roster can write to contact@rpgtools.co.

## 7. Reporting content

Anyone can report a public class, signed in or not, through the "Report" link on the class or the [report page](/report). A report needs:

- a reason,
- an explanation,
- the class,
- your name and email (optional for reports of child sexual abuse material), and
- a statement that you are reporting in good faith.

You receive a receipt by email and a message when the report is decided.

## 8. How we moderate

Reports are reviewed by a person, not an algorithm. The operator may:

- **dismiss** a report, telling the reporter why;
- **take down** a class: it disappears from search, pickers and every party's class pool, and no new characters can be rolled from it. Characters already rolled keep it;
- **remove the content**: in addition, the class's text is replaced with "[removed by moderation]" everywhere it appears, including in existing characters. This is used for illegal content and orders from authorities;
- **restore** a class after a successful dispute or a mistaken decision.

When a class is taken down or its content removed, the author is told why. A notice in the app and an email give the rule broken, what was done, and how to dispute it. While a class is taken down, its author can't republish it or save changes to it.

Reports of child sexual abuse material, threats to life or safety, or terrorist content may be passed to the police or the public prosecutor. The operator complies with removal orders from competent authorities, including the one-hour orders for terrorist content.

Records of moderation decisions and moderated content are kept as evidence. Reporters' names, emails and identifying details are removed 2 years after their report is resolved.

## 9. Disputing a decision

If you disagree with a decision about your class, use the "Dispute this decision" button on the notice in the app, or write to scvmrack-appeal@rpgtools.co. You can dispute a decision up to 3 times. A dispute is reviewed by a person, and you get an answer by email.

## 10. Limits

To keep the service usable for everyone:

- an account can hold up to 50 Custom Classes, archived ones included;
- a GM can make up to 5 classes public per day;
- saving, publishing and reporting are rate-limited per visitor.

When you reach a limit, the app tells you which one and what to do. The operator may change these limits; the current values are shown when you reach them.

## 11. Leaving

- You can delete your own Custom Classes. A class that characters were already rolled from is archived rather than deleted, so those characters keep working (§4).
- To remove your account and your content, write to contact@rpgtools.co from your account's email. Content that other users' characters already rely on stays in those characters (§4), without your name.
- [OWNER] Suggested: the operator may suspend an account that repeatedly breaks §5, after telling its owner why.

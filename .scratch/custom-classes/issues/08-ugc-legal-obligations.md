# What must a small EU-hosted hobby site do when hosting user-published content?

Type: research
Status: open

## Question

Primary sources only (EU Digital Services Act text, Polish implementing law, official guidance). What notice-and-action mechanism is required, what applies to copyright claims, what the obligations are on discovering CSAM or other illegal content (reporting, preservation, who to notify), and which obligations a micro/small provider is exempt from. Output feeds the moderation decisions.

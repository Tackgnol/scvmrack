# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Scvm Rack is for MORK BORG players and game masters who need a fast, readable character sheet during prep and at the table. They are maintaining doomed characters, equipment, combat values, notes, modifiers, and ownership state while staying in the game's harsh zine-like mood.

## Product Purpose

The app manages MORK BORG characters end to end: generation, editing, inventory, modifiers, notes, printing, anonymous ownership, authenticated accounts, and cross-device claims. Success means edits feel immediate, validation is clear before data leaves the browser, and the sheet remains usable under real play pressure.

## Brand Personality

Punk, brutal, doomed. The interface should feel like a xeroxed grimoire stamped onto a loud yellow page, but the task flow must remain direct and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Do not drift into generic SaaS Material polish, rounded admin dashboards, elegant dark mode, glassmorphism, gradient text, parchment-and-gold fantasy styling, soft pastel RPG storefronts, or decorative effects that compete with the sheet.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the sheet as the primary object: editing must stay immediate, dense, and scannable.
- Let feedback interrupt loudly when it protects user data.
- Keep flavor in the surface, not in broken affordances.
- Make ownership, saving, and validation states visible before users have to infer them.
- Treat print mode as its own functional artifact, not a weaker copy of the screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-visible interaction states. Validation must be available through normal form semantics, not only distant or clipped status badges. Motion should convey state quickly and respect reduced-motion preferences.

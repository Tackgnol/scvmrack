# Scvmrack for Owlbear Rodeo

Scvmrack puts a complete M™RK BORG character sheet beside your map. Players can manage their scvm without leaving the room, while game masters get a live party roster and an enemy board.

![The live Scvmrack party roster inside Owlbear Rodeo](https://scvmrack.rpgtools.co/owlbear/ForGameMasters1.png)

## Install

1. Open your Owlbear Rodeo room and go to **Settings  Manage Extensions**.
2. Add a custom extension using `https://scvmrack.rpgtools.co/manifest.json`.
3. Enable Scvmrack for the room, then open it from the action bar.

![Adding the Scvmrack manifest in Owlbear Rodeo](https://scvmrack.rpgtools.co/owlbear/Install1.png)

## For players

- Keep your character sheet open next to the map.
- Bind your active character to a token.
- Open the sheet attached to any bound token from its context menu.

![A player character sheet open beside the Owlbear Rodeo map](https://scvmrack.rpgtools.co/owlbear/ForPlayers1.png)

![Binding a Scvmrack character to an Owlbear Rodeo token](https://scvmrack.rpgtools.co/owlbear/ForPlayers2.png)

## For game masters

- See every linked player character in a live room roster.
- Promote the room roster into a persistent Scvmrack party.
- Create, edit, and place enemies on the scene from the enemy board.

![Managing enemies from Scvmrack inside Owlbear Rodeo](https://scvmrack.rpgtools.co/owlbear/ForGameMasters3.png)
